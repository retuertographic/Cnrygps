    How it works

    Three steps and you’re set

    01

    Your brand goes on vehicles already on the road

    Tourist tours, delivery, commercial fleets: vehicles that already move around the island every day.

    02

    Vehicles carry on as normal

    Nothing changes in how they operate. The GPS only records the route and does not interfere with anything.

    03

    You get a regular report

    Kilometres covered, routes, busiest areas and active days, per vehicle, as often as we agree with you.

    No real-time location

    We only share aggregated route reports, always with the express authorisation of the fleet operator and in compliance with current data protection law.
