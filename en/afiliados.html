    How it is calculated

    Commission is calculated on the discounted price your customer pays, not the public price. The more volume you bring in, the higher you climb, automatically.

    Frequently asked questions

    Do I have to buy devices to resell?

    No. There is no stock and no minimum purchase: each customer buys directly with your code.

    How long does the commission last?

    For as long as the customer you brought in keeps their subscription active.

    Can I sell under my own brand?

    No. The programme works with an affiliate code under the Canary GPS brand, not as white-label resale.
